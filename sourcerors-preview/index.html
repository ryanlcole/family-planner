WHAT EACH EXPERIENCE WOULD SOLVE

Four purposes. One human-first rule.

Work

Verified organizations and free direct applications, with human decisions instead of automated applicant exclusion.

Interviews

Human-issued invitations, candidate choice, accessible scheduling and reviewed fraud protections.

Products

Source-linked independent merchants and clear exact-versus-possible matches without inventing shipping information.

Account

Separate permissions for applications, shopping, and human communication, with no cross-purpose data sale.

THE MISSION · THE PLAN · THE EVIDENCE

Why build Sourcerors?

When automated content and opaque rankings separate people from sources, it's harder to tell who's hiring, who's selling, and who's actually speaking. Sourcerors is designed to make that connection direct again.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Discovery shouldn't require trusting a stranger's algorithm.

People waste time on unverifiable job posts, businesses face automated applicants, and shoppers struggle to find independent makers behind products they saw online.

02 · THE SOLUTION

AI helps you search. Humans represent themselves.

Find attributable companies, direct applications, human-issued interviews, and seller-linked products. AI may organize and explain information, but cannot pose as an applicant, employer, customer, or seller.

03 · TODAY'S EVIDENCE

A real beginning, not a finished network.

This public page is an interactive sample-data demonstration. A separate private application has a tested account, application, interview, and merchant-catalog pilot. Neither proves a public launch or existing real marketplace participants.

04 · WHAT SUPPORT WOULD BUILD

Fund milestones that can be checked.

Secure hosting and database; human business verification; applicant privacy and fraud controls; accessible real-device testing; authorized product-source coverage; and, only after legal review, optional interview compensation.
